Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Operator: Robin Calamatta (Ontario, Canada) · Contact: the contact listed on the page where you got Hakma

Hakma collects as little personal information as possible. It works fully offline with no account — then nothing about you leaves your device. This policy covers the Hakma website and the Hakma Android app (the app simply opens the same game).

On your device

Save files, settings and (if you log in) a session token are stored in the app's / browser's local storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes them.

What we collect if you create an account

Accounts are optional and only used for cloud saves.

  • Account: username, email address, a salted and hashed password (never the password itself), and when you accepted the Terms.
  • Game data: cloud copies of your save files (names you typed, monsters, progress, quiz statistics) and when they were last updated.
  • Sessions: hashed login tokens and when they were last used.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or device identifiers, and Hakma contains no ads, no third-party analytics and no trackers.

Why we use it

Only to run your account, back up and sync your saves between devices, keep the service secure and prevent abuse. We never sell or share personal information for advertising.

Where it is stored

Account and cloud-save data is stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers and D1), which processes it on our behalf. Like any web host, Cloudflare may briefly log technical request data such as IP addresses for security and reliability. Data is always sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Who can see it

Only you and the operator. No other players can see your account details or saves.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all cloud saves at any time in the game: Menu → ☁ Account & Cloud → Delete my account. See how to delete your account for other ways. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Your rights

Under Canada's PIPEDA (and, where they apply, laws such as the GDPR or CCPA) you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your information and withdraw consent. Signed-in players can download their data in the game (☁ Account & Cloud → Download my data). For anything else, contact us at the address above.

Children

Accounts are for people 13 and over, or for younger children only when a parent or guardian creates and supervises the account. Younger players can enjoy the whole game offline without an account. If you believe we hold a child's data without that consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA-256 and a unique salt, session tokens are stored only as hashes, and all traffic uses HTTPS. Please use a unique password.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
