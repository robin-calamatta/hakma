Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Operator: Robin Calamatta (Ontario, Canada) · Contact: the contact listed on the page where you got Hakma

Hakma collects as little personal information as possible. The game works fully offline with no account; then nothing leaves your device.

On your device

Save files, settings and (if you log in) your session token are stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them.

What we collect if you create an account

  • Account: username, email address, a salted and hashed password (never the password itself), your friend code, and when you accepted the Terms.
  • Game data: cloud copies of your save files (player names you typed, monsters, progress, quiz statistics).
  • Social: friends, friend requests and party membership. Party chat is relayed live and is not stored.
  • Activity and security logs: login times, when and how long you are online, your in-game area while online, IP address, browser user-agent, failed logins, and admin actions on your account.

Why we use it

To run your account, sync saves, show friends when you're online, run co-op parties, keep the service secure, prevent abuse, enforce the Terms and understand overall usage. We do not sell personal information, show ads, or use third-party trackers or analytics.

Who can see it

Friends and party members see your username, online status, last-seen time and in-game location. Administrators of this Hakma server can see account details, saves, sessions, IP addresses and logs for moderation and support. If you play on someone else's Hakma server, its operator is responsible for your data.

Retention

Account data is kept while your account exists; security logs for up to 24 months. Deleting an account deletes its saves, friendships, tokens and sessions; records of admin actions may be kept without your save data.

Your rights

Under Canada's PIPEDA (and, where they apply, laws such as the GDPR or CCPA) you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your information and withdraw consent. Signed-in players can download their data from /api/me/export. For other requests, write to the contact email shown at the top of this page (contact). You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Accounts are for people 13 and over or for children whose parent or guardian creates and supervises the account. If you believe we hold a younger child's data without that consent, contact us and we will delete it. Younger players can enjoy the full game offline without an account.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, session tokens are stored only as hashes, and Hakma should be served over HTTPS. Please use a unique password.
