Delete your Hakma account

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Operator: Robin Calamatta (Ontario, Canada) · Contact: the contact listed on the page where you got Hakma

Hakma accounts are optional and only used for cloud saves. You can delete your account and all of its cloud saves at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

In the Android app or on the website

  1. Open Hakma and load any save.
  2. Open the Menu and choose ☁ Account & Cloud (log in if asked).
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

You can also do this on the website at the Hakma web game — it is the same account.

If you can't log in

Email us at the contact address at the top of this page from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Hakma account” and your username. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your username, email address and hashed password
  • All cloud save files
  • All login sessions

Nothing is kept afterwards. Saves stored only on your own device are not affected — uninstall the app or clear the site's data to remove them.
